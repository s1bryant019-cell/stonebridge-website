# Stonebridge — complete active-page Redundancy & Clarity editorial review
**Review basis:** currently integrated 26 root HTML entries, of which 24 render as independent active pages. `fit-safety-check.html` redirects to New Clients; `psychotherapy.html` redirects to Services. Those two do not have independently visible text and are not scored as stand-alone pages.

**Scope:** main content headings, supporting paragraphs, lists and calls to action, inspected within the current reading order and against the desktop/mobile screenshot context. Conceptual overlap was screened with full-text extraction and compared in its real user-facing section context. This is an editorial recommendation register, **not permission to alter clinical, safety, legal, insurance, fees, credential, or scheduling statements.** Some short navigation labels are intentionally recurring.

## Classification rubric
- **KEEP / necessary reinforcement:** repeated concept prevents a missed clinical, safety, eligibility or financial qualification or clarifies a separate audience.
- **KEEP / useful reminder:** repeats a topic but adds a different decision, clinician fit, card context or next step.
- **REVIEW / candidate consolidation:** repeat has limited incremental meaning; owner should decide whether removing one occurrence preserves its necessary qualifications. Copy remains unchanged.
- **KEEP / no demonstrated material duplication:** full active page text reviewed; repetition is navigational or genuinely section-specific. Absence of a strong match is not a claim that the page is beyond all editorial refinement.

## Scoring implications
The previous integrated scorecard did not evaluate enough complete page text to score most Redundancy & Clarity cells. This review supplies the missing source-based evidence. The **strict 9.5 threshold** remains: pages with even meaningful but improvable conceptual overlap may score below 9.5; this is not permission to remove clinically necessary reinforcement. The two retired URLs remain **N/V** as independent page-level content.
The revised scorecard should score all 24 actively visible pages separately on mobile/desktop, with evidence and status. Different scroll lengths can influence mobile *reading experience*, but text repetition itself does not change merely because the viewport does.

## Release control
This document contains **recommendations only**. No website copy is modified. Each future consolidation requires its own approved editorial change, preserving Stonebridge's clinical, safety, fee, insurer, legal, supervision and applicant-data statements.
