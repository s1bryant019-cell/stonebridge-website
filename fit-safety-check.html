New-client screening

Fit & Safety Check

This brief check helps Stonebridge determine whether you can proceed directly to a first-appointment request or whether we need to clarify something first. It is not a diagnosis, comprehensive assessment, or guarantee of acceptance for treatment.

Do not use this form for an emergency.

If you are experiencing a medical or psychiatric emergency, believe you may harm yourself or someone else, or feel unable to remain safe, call 911, go to the nearest emergency department, call or text 988, or use local crisis services.

Please answer only what is asked here. Stonebridge intentionally does not collect a broad clinical history through this screening form.
