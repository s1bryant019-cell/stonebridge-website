Getting started

Starting Therapy at Stonebridge

You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out. Here's how to begin.

01

Choose Your Starting Point

Review available appointments and request a time, or request a free 10–15 minute consultation if you would prefer to speak with someone first.

02

We'll Review Your Request

Stonebridge reviews appointment requests to confirm clinician availability and whether the requested service is appropriate. Consultation requests are followed up separately to arrange a conversation.

03

Begin With a Clear Plan

Once your appointment is confirmed, you'll receive the information needed to prepare for your first session. Treatment begins with an opportunity to discuss your concerns, background, and goals.

Ready to Begin?

Whether you're ready to request your first appointment or would prefer a brief conversation before deciding, Stonebridge offers a clear way to take the next step.
