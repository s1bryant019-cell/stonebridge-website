Trauma-informed care

When difficult experiences still feel active in the present.

Therapy can address how difficult experiences continue to shape emotions, relationships, coping, physical activation, avoidance, or shutdown.

01

Hyperarousal and overwhelm

Feeling on alert, reactive, tense, easily overwhelmed, or unable to settle after stress.

02

Avoidance and disconnection

Pulling away from memories, emotions, situations, relationships, or parts of yourself because they feel difficult to approach.

03

Recurring protective patterns

Ways of coping that may once have helped but now contribute to shame, conflict, shutdown, substance use, or other difficulties.
