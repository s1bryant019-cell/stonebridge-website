Services

Different needs call for different forms of care.

Stonebridge provides outpatient telehealth psychotherapy for adults and adolescents, couples, families, parents, and groups. The service format should fit who is seeking support and where the difficulty is occurring.

Ways to work with Stonebridge

Care organized around the person, relationship, or system in the room.

You do not need to know the exact format before reaching out. The consultation can help clarify which service best fits the request.

Format and scope

Individual, couples, family, and parent-focused services vary according to who is participating and the treatment need. Stonebridge provides outpatient telehealth care. Emergency services, psychological testing, legal or court-related evaluations, and needs requiring a higher level of care require different services.
