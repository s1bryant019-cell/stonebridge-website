The Lobby

Teen group therapy through cooperative play.

A small therapist-led online group using cooperative Stardew Valley gameplay to practice communication, flexibility, collaboration, participation, and connection.

The game is the medium. The psychotherapy is the service.

How cooperative gameplay helps

A shared activity creates something real to work with.

Cooperative play gives the group something concrete to navigate together — planning, solving for help, adapting to different ideas, making mistakes, and repairing friction. Those moments become natural opportunities to practice skills that carry into school, friendships, and home.

Communication & perspective-taking

Practice expressing ideas, listening, and considering different points of view.

Collaboration & flexibility

Navigate different styles, share decisions, and adjust when plans change.

Frustration tolerance & repair

Work through setbacks, regulate emotions, and rebuild after conflict.

Who it’s designed for

Two possible areas of clinical focus.

Stonebridge organizes this group around common needs among adolescents who benefit from structured, social, interest-based experiences.

Social connection

For teens who want support with friendship skills, feeling more comfortable in social settings, and building confidence with peers.

Executive functioning

For teens who benefit from structure and support with flexibility, organization, follow-through, and emotional regulation.

Clinical scope: This specialty group is intended for adolescents appropriate for routine outpatient telehealth group psychotherapy. Stonebridge may recommend a different service when current safety or monitoring needs exceed the group format.

How enrollment works

From inquiry to a closed cohort.

1

Inquiry

Request a consultation and share general information about your teen.

2

Screening consultation

We’ll discuss goals, answer questions, and assess whether the group’s purpose and format are appropriate for your teen.

3

Cohort placement

If appropriate, Stonebridge confirms placement as a cohort comes together. An inquiry does not guarantee placement or a specific start date.

Before joining

A few practical expectations.

Consistent participation

Teens are expected to attend regularly and be an active, respectful participant.

Comfort with group format

This group works best for teens who are open to real-time conversation and collaboration.

Basic tech setup

A computer, stable internet connection, and the ability to use the game platform are required.

Privacy & consent: Guardian consent and adolescent assent are required. Screenshots, recording, streaming, and sharing other participants’ information are not permitted.
