For legal professionals

Available forensic services

Led by Dr. Stephen W. Bryant, PsyD, a licensed clinical psychologist with training in clinical and forensic psychology. Engagements are reviewed individually for professional role, competence, scope, and availability.

Forensic Records Review

Professional review and analysis of clinical records, psychological evaluations, treatment documentation, and other materials relevant to legal proceedings. Services may include written summaries, identification of relevant psychological findings, and consultation regarding the strengths and limitations of available documentation.

Attorney and Guardian ad Litem (GAL) Consultation

Professional consultation with attorneys, guardians ad litem, and other legal professionals regarding psychological issues. When consultation concerns an existing psychotherapy patient, appropriate authorization or another lawful basis for disclosure is required. Such consultation remains within the treating psychologist's clinical role and does not constitute an independent forensic evaluation.

Forensic Case Consultation

Consultation concerning psychological evidence, relevant research, existing clinical assessments, and psychological questions arising in legal proceedings. Services are provided within the psychologist's areas of professional competence.

Expert Witness Services

Expert consultation, preparation, depositions, and courtroom testimony concerning psychological matters within the psychologist's professional expertise. Expert opinions are based on appropriately reviewed information and are subject to professional, ethical, and evidentiary limitations. Expert witness engagements are reviewed individually before acceptance.

Professional judgment

Our Approach

Forensic psychology requires a clear understanding of the distinction between clinical treatment and legal evaluation.

Stonebridge emphasizes professional objectivity, appropriate application of psychological research, careful review of available information, and transparent communication about the limitations of psychological opinions.

Consultations and expert services are accepted only when they fall within the psychologist's professional competence and an appropriate evidentiary foundation is available.

Before engagement

Referral and Consultation Process

Attorneys, GALs, and other legal professionals may contact Stonebridge to discuss a potential consultation or referral.

Before accepting an engagement, the practice reviews the requested services, potential conflicts of interest, the professional role, anticipated time requirements, and financial arrangements.

Formal engagements are subject to separate written agreements.

Role clarity

Important Professional Boundaries

Forensic services are separate from psychotherapy. Consultation concerning an existing therapy patient is limited to the treating psychologist's role and the scope of legally permitted disclosure.

Records-based consultation and expert testimony do not automatically constitute an independent psychological evaluation. Opinions are limited by the available information and applicable professional standards.

Psychological testing and independent forensic psychological evaluations are not currently offered.

Services do not guarantee a particular legal or clinical outcome.
