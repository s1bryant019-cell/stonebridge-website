Professional services

Available forensic services

Led by Dr. Stephen W. Bryant, PsyD, a licensed clinical psychologist with education and training in clinical and forensic psychology.

Forensic Records Review

Review and analysis of clinical records, psychological evaluations, and treatment documentation relevant to legal matters. Work may include written summaries and consideration of the strengths and limitations of available records.

Attorney and Guardian ad Litem (GAL) Consultation

Professional consultation with attorneys, guardians ad litem, and other legal professionals concerning psychological issues.

Forensic Case Consultation

Consultation regarding psychological evidence, relevant research, existing assessments, and psychological questions arising in legal proceedings.

Expert Witness Services

Expert consultation, preparation, depositions, and courtroom testimony concerning psychological matters within Dr. Bryant's professional expertise.

Process & professional boundaries

How engagements work

Each request is reviewed for the referral question, conflicts of interest, professional role, available information, timing, and financial arrangements. Accepted engagements are governed by a separate written agreement.

Work is conducted objectively and only within professional competence, with opinions grounded in appropriately reviewed information, relevant research, and applicable ethical and evidentiary standards. Opinions acknowledge their limitations and do not guarantee a legal or clinical outcome.

These services are separate from psychotherapy. Consultation regarding an existing therapy patient requires appropriate authorization or another lawful basis for disclosure and remains within the treating psychologist's role. Records review and expert testimony are not independent psychological evaluations. Psychological testing and independent forensic psychological evaluations are not currently offered.
