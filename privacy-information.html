Privacy & Information Use

This page explains how information submitted through the Stonebridge website consultation form is used and what belongs in that form.

Website consultation form Privacy & Information Use Keep clinical details brief

Stonebridge uses the website consultation form to understand what a prospective client is looking for, determine how to respond, and support next-step coordination. The form is intentionally limited: it is not a clinical intake, a place to upload records, or a substitute for emergency care.

Information submitted through the consultation form

The form asks for identifying and contact information, the type of service you are seeking, general availability and payment preferences when you choose to provide them, and a brief description of what brings you to Stonebridge.

Date of birth helps Stonebridge confirm who it is communicating with and supports accurate client-record and portal setup if care moves forward.

How the information is used

Information submitted through the form is used to respond to the consultation request, review fit and availability, and coordinate next steps. When appropriate, it may also support setup of the prospective client’s record and secure portal workflow if care moves forward.

Stonebridge may follow up using the contact information provided in the request.

What not to submit here

Please keep the website form brief. Do not use it to submit detailed clinical history, treatment records, assessment reports, medication lists, extensive trauma history, or other documents.

If care moves forward, more detailed clinical information and records can be shared through the appropriate secure process.

Consultation requests are not appointments

Submitting the website form sends a consultation request. It does not schedule or confirm an appointment and does not establish an emergency-response channel.

Emergency and crisis needs

If you are in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department. For crisis support, call or text 988.

Questions about website information use

For questions about information submitted through this website, contact Stonebridge at info@stonebridgepsychgroup.com or (773) 417-1688.

Scope of this page

This page is a concise explanation of the website consultation workflow. It does not make technical or legal claims about security, confidentiality, or regulatory compliance beyond the practices described here.
