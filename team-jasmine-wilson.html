Clinician Profile

Jasmine Wilson

LPC · Licensed Professional Counselor
Postgraduate Clinical Associate

Jasmine provides individual psychotherapy for adolescents age 12+ and adults navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, stress, emotional overwhelm, life transitions, and patterns that have become difficult to keep carrying alone.

Her style is warm, collaborative, reflective, and direct. She creates room to understand what is happening while also helping clients notice patterns, strengthen coping, and develop more workable ways of responding.

Jasmine provides services under the clinical supervision of Dr. Stephen W. Bryant, PsyD, LCPC.

Clinical focus

Areas Jasmine commonly works with.

Jasmine works with people who may understand that something is wrong—or even understand the pattern intellectually—but still find that stress, past experiences, difficult emotions, or familiar responses continue to shape daily life.

01

Trauma and complex stress

Past experiences can continue to affect how safe a person feels, how quickly the body responds to stress, what happens in relationships, and how difficult emotions are managed. Jasmine works with trauma-related distress, emotional reactivity, avoidance, shame, and protective patterns that are no longer working as well.

02

Anxiety, depression, and emotional regulation

Persistent worry, overthinking, low mood, irritability, shutdown, overwhelm, burnout, or difficulty recovering after stressful experiences can make everyday life increasingly hard to manage. Therapy can help make these responses clearer and more workable.

03

Life transitions, identity, and self-worth

Changes in school, work, relationships, family roles, health, independence, or other parts of life can bring questions about identity, direction, confidence, and what comes next. Jasmine works with transitions, self-criticism, self-esteem concerns, and identity development.

Broader care may include

Grief and loss, ADHD-related concerns, relationship patterns, family stress, parenting concerns, behavioral concerns, and substance-use concerns when a harm-reduction approach is clinically appropriate.

Clinical approach

Warm enough to feel human. Direct enough to be useful.

Jasmine works from a person-centered foundation. Her approach is collaborative and attentive to the client’s experience rather than built around forcing every concern into a predetermined formula. She may integrate trauma-informed, body-aware, reflective, and skills-based work according to the person, the concern, and what is most useful in the moment.

01

Person-centered and collaborative

Clients are active participants in the work. Jasmine emphasizes curiosity, respect, and understanding what an experience means to the individual rather than assuming the same approach will fit everyone.

02

Trauma-informed and body-aware

Therapy may pay attention to physical activation, shutdown, emotional responses, protective patterns, and the ways the body responds when something feels threatening or overwhelming.

03

Reflective and practical

Understanding a pattern can matter, but insight by itself does not always change it. Jasmine can combine reflection with practical skills intended to help clients respond differently outside of therapy.

04

Warm and direct

Jasmine brings warmth and compassion to the therapeutic relationship while remaining willing to name patterns, ask difficult questions, and help clients look more closely at what may be keeping them stuck.

Who may find this work helpful

You do not need to have everything figured out before reaching out.

Jasmine may be a good person to consult with if:

  • Something from the past still seems to show up in the present.
  • Stress or emotions have become harder to regulate.
  • Anxiety, low mood, burnout, or self-criticism are taking up too much space.
  • A transition has left you unsure of yourself or what comes next.
  • You recognize a pattern in relationships or coping but have difficulty changing it.
  • You want therapy that feels compassionate without feeling vague or passive.
Professional background

Clinical training informed by work across behavioral health and community settings.

Jasmine’s professional background includes behavioral health assessment and treatment, community-based mental health care, crisis intervention, trauma-informed services, and work with people navigating significant health, housing, relationship, and life stressors.

Her experience has included work with adolescents, young adults, and adults across behavioral health, community, and youth-service settings. That broader experience informs a clinical approach that considers symptoms alongside the realities of a person’s relationships, environment, resources, identity, and daily life.

Scope of care

Outpatient care within a clear clinical setting.

Jasmine provides outpatient telehealth psychotherapy within her approved Stonebridge role for clients located in Illinois.

Stonebridge is not an emergency or crisis service and does not provide psychological testing, forensic evaluation, or court-related evaluation through this care pathway.

Clinical needs, risk level, age, treatment format, clinician scope, and appropriateness for telehealth are considered when determining fit. When another service or level of care appears more appropriate, Stonebridge may discuss referral direction when possible.
