Clinician Profile

Jasmine Wilson

LPC · Licensed Professional Counselor
Postgraduate Clinical Associate

Jasmine provides individual psychotherapy for adolescents age 12+ and adults navigating trauma, anxiety, depression, stress, emotional overwhelm, life transitions, and recurring concerns related to relationships or self-worth. Her style is warm, collaborative, reflective, and direct.

Jasmine provides services under the clinical supervision of Dr. Stephen W. Bryant, PsyD, LCPC.

Clinical focus

Areas Jasmine commonly works with.

Focused areas of care within a broader outpatient practice.

01

Trauma and complex stress

Trauma-related distress, emotional reactivity, avoidance, shame, and protective patterns that are no longer working as well.

02

Anxiety, depression, and regulation

Persistent worry, overthinking, low mood, irritability, shutdown, overwhelm, burnout, and difficulty recovering after stress.

03

Life transitions, identity, and self-worth

Changes in school, work, relationships, family roles, health, independence, confidence, identity, and direction.

Broader care may include

Grief and loss, ADHD-related concerns, relationship patterns, family stress, parenting concerns, behavioral concerns, and substance-use concerns when a harm-reduction approach is clinically appropriate.

Clinical approach

Warm, collaborative, and direct.

Jasmine works from a person-centered foundation and may integrate trauma-informed, body-aware, reflective, and skills-based work according to the person and the concern.

01

Person-centered and collaborative

Therapy emphasizes curiosity, respect, and understanding what an experience means to the individual rather than assuming the same approach fits everyone.

02

Trauma-informed and body-aware

Work may attend to physical activation, shutdown, emotional responses, and protective patterns when stress or past experiences remain active in the present.

03

Reflective and practical

Insight is paired with practical skills when useful so clients can respond differently outside the therapy session.

Who may find this work helpful

You do not need everything figured out before reaching out.

  • Something from the past still seems to show up in the present.
  • Stress, anxiety, low mood, or emotions have become harder to manage.
  • A transition has left you unsure of yourself or what comes next.
  • You want therapy that feels compassionate without feeling vague or passive.
Professional background

Jasmine’s background includes behavioral health assessment and treatment, community-based mental health care, crisis intervention, trauma-informed services, and work with adolescents and adults navigating significant life stressors.
