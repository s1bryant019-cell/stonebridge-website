Clinical approach

Warm, collaborative, and direct.

Jasmine works from a person-centered foundation and integrates trauma-informed, body-aware, reflective, and skills-based approaches.

01

Person-centered and collaborative

Therapy emphasizes curiosity, respect, and an approach shaped to the individual.

02

Trauma-informed and body-aware

The work may attend to activation, shutdown, emotional responses, and protective patterns.

03

Reflective and practical

Insight can be paired with practical skills for use outside therapy.

Who may find this work helpful

You do not need everything figured out before reaching out.

  • Past experiences still shape the present.
  • Stress, anxiety, low mood, or emotions feel harder to manage.
  • A transition has left you unsure of yourself or what comes next.
