Common questions

Before you reach out.

Coverage is plan-specific and cannot be guaranteed in advance.

Does Stonebridge accept BCBS Illinois?

Stonebridge currently identifies BCBS Illinois commercial PPO, Blue Choice, and Blue High Performance as active plans. Eligibility is confirmed when relevant.

How do I know what my plan will cover?

Deductibles, copays, coinsurance, and other client responsibility vary by plan. Available benefit information is reviewed before treatment begins.

Do I need to choose a therapist before contacting Stonebridge?

No. TherapyPortal can offer clinician selection, including Any Available Clinician where configured. Consultation remains available if you want help choosing.

Outpatient scope

Stonebridge provides outpatient telehealth psychotherapy. Emergency or crisis care and needs requiring a higher level of care require different resources. View Fees & Insurance.
