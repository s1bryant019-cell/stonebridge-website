Common questions

Before you reach out.

Does Stonebridge accept BCBS Illinois?

Stonebridge currently works with applicable BCBS Illinois commercial PPO, Blue Choice, and Blue High Performance plans. Network status and benefits vary by plan.

Do I need to know exactly what my insurance covers first?

No. Applicable insurance information can be reviewed before treatment begins. Benefits and client responsibility vary by plan.

Do I need to choose a therapist before requesting an appointment?

No. Stonebridge can review therapist fit and availability as part of the new-client process.

What if I want to speak with someone before requesting an appointment?

You may request a free 10–15 minute consultation before deciding whether to proceed. Request a Free Consultation.
