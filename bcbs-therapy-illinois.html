Common questions

Before you reach out.

Coverage details are plan-specific. These answers describe Stonebridge’s current process rather than guaranteeing benefits under a particular policy.

Does Stonebridge accept BCBS Illinois?

Stonebridge currently identifies BCBS Illinois commercial PPO, Blue Choice, and Blue High Performance as active plans. Network eligibility is confirmed when relevant.

How do I know what my plan will cover?

Benefits, deductible status, copays, coinsurance, and other client responsibility can vary. Stonebridge reviews available benefit information before treatment begins.

Do I need to choose a therapist before contacting Stonebridge?

No. The consultation process is designed to review clinical needs, clinician fit, availability, and service scope before treatment begins.

Outpatient scope

Stonebridge provides outpatient telehealth psychotherapy. Telehealth appropriateness and clinical fit are reviewed before treatment. Emergency or crisis care and needs requiring a higher level of care require different resources. View Fees & Insurance.

Questions about fit or coverage?

Start with a brief consultation. Stonebridge will review what you are looking for, current availability, service fit, and next steps.
